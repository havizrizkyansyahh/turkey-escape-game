# Rank push v3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** v3 adds the spec's "v3" items on top of v2, which is already submitted:

- a corn decoy, a real choice that also beats the ambush;
- an unlockable disguise that Tom wears in play and in every screenshot.

v3 is submitted before 2026-11-29T02:00Z, and well before it.

**Architecture:**

- `decoys.js`: a pure module, the same pattern as `ambush.js`. It owns the decoy count, the thrown cob and the farmer's reaction to it.
- `wardrobe.js`: maps owned medals to hats. `turkey-art.js` draws the hat Tom has on.
- HUD, screens and shots only read this state.

**Tech Stack:** As in v2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-rank-push-design.md`, section "v3".

## Global Constraints

- Same as v2: static files only, real-render screenshots of at most 256 KiB each, an identical dossier on two runs, a deterministic simulation, and no attribution lines in commits.
- v2's cover composition stays. v3 only adds to the scenes, so v3 is never a step back from v2 in the judge's view.
- Tuning bar: good bot at least 43 of 60 escapes with 0 crashes, and the lazy bot caught before 400 m.

## Review Focus

- **Q is pressed with 0 decoys.** Nothing is thrown. A "NO DECOYS" popup shows, and no event repeats every frame.
- **A decoy lands while the farmer is searching or stunned.** He ignores it until he chases again, then keeps ignoring it, because it expires.
- **Saved bests from v2 that carry no `hat` field.** They default to no hat.

### Task 1: Corn decoy

**Files:**
- Create: `game/src/decoys.js`
- Create: `test/decoys.test.js`
- Modify: `config.js` (add `DECOY_TUNING`), `input.js` (map Q to `decoyPressed`), `run.js`, `farmer.js` (add state `distracted`), `ambush.js` (a decoy pulls him out of the cornering stance), `hud.js` (decoy count and a Q key cap), `farmer-art.js` (pose for `distracted`)

**Interfaces:**
- `DECOY_TUNING = { start: 1, max: 3, cornPerDecoy: 25, throwSpeed: 520, distractSeconds: 1.5, lureRange: 700, lifeSeconds: 6 }`
- `throwDecoy(run) -> boolean`: spends one decoy and adds a `decoy` entity that flies toward the farmer (behind Tom, or ahead of him in an ambush) and lands on the floor.
- `updateDecoys(run, dt)`:
  - **Chasing:** a farmer within `lureRange` of a landed cob walks to it, and is `distracted` for 1.5 s ("Ooh! Sweet corn!"). The cob is then gone, and the event is `decoyTaken`.
  - **Cornering or swipe:** if a cob lands within 160 px of him, he dives for it. His state becomes `distracted`, the ambush finishes, and Tom gets "DECOYED! +80".
- `run.decoys`: a number. Eating 25 corn adds one decoy, up to 3. The stat `decoysUsed` counts throws.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `test/decoys.test.js`:
  - `a decoy thrown behind Tom stops a chasing farmer`: the farmer state becomes `distracted` within 2 s, and the gap grows.
  - `a decoy beats an ambush`: with `startAmbush(high)`, throw while the farmer is 300 px ahead, then idle controls. Expected: `turkey.lives === 3`, and the events include `decoyTaken`.
  - `no decoys, no throw`: `run.decoys = 0`, and `throwDecoy` returns `false`.
  - `corn earns decoys`: 25 corn adds 1 decoy, capped at 3.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run `node --test test/*.test.js`. Expected: FAIL, because `decoys.js` is missing.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement the decoy, add Q to the key strip and the help card, and teach the good bot to throw one when the farmer is within 200 px of Tom and Tom has a decoy.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run `node --test test/*.test.js` and `node tools/simulate-runs.ts 60`. Expected: PASS, and the tuning bar holds.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Commit with the message `feat: corn decoys distract the farmer, even mid-ambush`.
